About Me

My name is Atika Oubellil and I am currently a student at Algonquin College in the IMD program. This program is exciting because it allows me to explore digital creation in many forms, ranging from web programming to cutting-edge graphic design. My goal is to become an expert in interactive design by the end of my studies.

As a first-year student, I make it a priority to practice my HTML skills every day. It is essential to understand the structure of the web to build sites that are both aesthetic and functional. Each new project is an opportunity for me to learn and push the boundaries of my technological creativity.
